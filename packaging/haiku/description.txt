Ageku (挙句, the closing verse of a renku) is a web browser for Haiku and Renku OS with a native Haiku interface, built on the Unified XUL Platform (UXP). It is an independent community project; please report problems at https://github.com/idumych/ageku/issues. Optional packages, loaded at run time when installed: mesa (libOSMesa) for WebGL; ffmpeg6 or ffmpeg8 (libavcodec) for H.264 video and AAC audio (VP8, VP9, AV1 and Opus play without it); myspell or myspell_<language> for spell checking.
